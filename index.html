The product

01 — NayHotel

NayHotel — the operating intelligence platform for hotels.

NayHotel turns a hotel's operating playbook into running software: every role, every KPI, every red flag, every meeting rhythm — encoded, monitored, and escalated. Built for owners and operators who cannot wait for a beta to know the system is right.

Layer 01

HotelCore

The operating system of record — 74 roles, 238 KPIs, 104 red flags with severity ladders and prescribed solutions, 48 meeting rhythms, 99 core templates, and 28 SLAs, compiled from the Change the Jacket consulting methodology.

Layer 02

HotelIntelligence

A deterministic screening engine that evaluates live property metrics against the workbook's own thresholds — no AI in the decision path. Every rule is gated by a 48-test scenario suite, so correctness is proven before deployment, not during it.

Layer 03

HotelIntelligenceCore — optional

An AI enhancement layer that subscribes to the screening event stream: richer rendering, extended detection, free-text triage. Metered per-inference, priced separately, and never required for the platform to function.

How licensing works

02 — One template, every licensee
01

License, not transfer

Your company receives a revocable, per-property subscription license to operate the platform. SITechnologies retains all ownership — source code, systems, models, interfaces, and the accumulated platform data layer. Termination ends use of the marks and the service; it never transfers anything.

02

Your own tenant address

Every licensee operates from a dedicated tenant host on SITechnologies infrastructure, served from our domains only — never under a client domain. Authentication is issued from the tenant host: whoever controls the login, owns the user. NayHotel.{your-company}.SITechnologies.io

03

Co-branded, we own the login

The tenant login page carries your brand beside ours and presents the platform access agreement: every user acknowledges that the platform, its APIs, its AI systems, and all transmitted data layers are the property rights of SITechnologies. Acceptance is logged per user, per terms version.

04

Metered from day one

Calls are logged per tenant, per function. The metering record is the pricing evidence for renewals and the usage basis for optional AI inference — and it is exactly what the next licensee's terms will be built on.

Ownership & rights

03 — The standing terms
Everything the platform does — every API response, every model call, every byte transmitted through it — runs on property owned by SITechnologies.
Platform IP

NayHotel and all its layers — software, UI/UX, aggregation logic, rule engines, template libraries, documentation — are the exclusive property of SITechnologies.

AI & model rights

All AI systems, LLM integrations, prompts, weights, outputs generated through the platform's systems, and the event streams that feed them remain SITechnologies property, whether used at runtime or exported in reports.

Data transference

Data transmitted through the platform — including via API, AI, or LLM calls — is processed under license. The licensee receives a limited right to use outputs in their business; no right to extract, resell, or re-platform the data layer itself.

Attribution

"NayHotel — powered by SITechnologies.io" appears on login, application, reports, and client-facing exports. Removal or obscuring of attribution is a material breach.

Marks

Licensees may not register or use domains, handles, or marks containing "SITechnologies", "NayHotel", or confusingly similar variants, anywhere in the world.

Personal data

Where platform data includes personal data, SITechnologies acts as processor under a data processing addendum; ownership-style rights apply to the platform and its outputs, never to the underlying persons' statutory privacy rights.
