Software, owned outright.

SITechnologies builds and owns software platforms — including NayHotel, the hotel operations intelligence platform — and licenses them to operating companies. The platform stays with the licensor. The value goes to the licensee.

01 — The model

Own the platform. License the outcome.

Traditional SaaS rents you access and can raise the price. Custom dev hands you code you can't maintain. The licensing model sits between them: SITechnologies owns the platform, maintains it, and extends it — operating companies license it for their vertical, their brand, their data.
